Dear Dr. Hachem-Vermette,

The front page update is progressing. Existing Neighborhoods now offers 1983 and Standard (NECB 2017), and New Neighborhoods offers HPENV only. 
I attached a short walkthrough of the first five pages for each pathway.

While testing, I saw one point I would like to confirm with you. In the simulations, the energy measures are cumulative: Standard, then HPENV, then space heat pump, then heat pump for DHW, then efficient appliances. So on the Existing pathway, as soon as a heat pump is selected in Layer 2, the result already includes the HPENV. For example, for RC-R in Montreal, Existing and New both give 60.6 kWh/m²·yr with heat pump, DHW and appliances. Existing stays without HPENV only when no heat pump is selected.

I see two options:
1. Keep the data as it is, and add a clear note on the site that heat pump results on the Existing pathway include the HPENV.
2. Simulate heat pump measures on the Standard envelope without HPENV, so Existing and New give different results. This needs new simulation runs.

I would suggest option 1 for now, and option 2 later if needed. Please let me know which one you prefer.
